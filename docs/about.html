Our Story

Built from a passion for African innovation

Founded by Austin Oduma, an entrepreneur and innovator with a passion for creating positive global impact, Oduma Corp seeks to transform ideas into impactful solutions. With deep local roots and a global outlook, we provide the tools and a platform for innovators to bring their ideas to life.

We are dedicated to helping individuals and communities improve their livelihoods by fostering innovations and inventions — unlocking new opportunities for personal and economic growth across Africa.

Our Vision

Leading Africa's innovation landscape

We envision Oduma Corp as a leading force in Africa's innovation landscape — championing a culture of creativity, invention, and entrepreneurship. We are committed to nurturing local talent and supporting businesses to grow and thrive in a competitive global market.

Through our platform, we provide the tools, support, and connections needed to shape a brighter, more inventive future for Kenya and the continent.
