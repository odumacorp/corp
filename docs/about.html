Our story

Built from a passion for African innovation

Founded by Austin Oduma, an entrepreneur with a passion for creating positive global impact, Oduma Corp seeks to transform ideas into impactful solutions. With deep local roots and a global outlook, we provide the tools and a platform for innovators to bring their ideas to life.

We are dedicated to helping individuals and communities improve their livelihoods by fostering innovation and invention, unlocking new opportunities for personal and economic growth across Africa.

Our vision

Leading Africa's innovation landscape

We envision Oduma Corp as a leading force in Africa's innovation landscape, championing a culture of creativity, invention and entrepreneurship. We are committed to nurturing local talent and supporting businesses to grow and thrive in a competitive global market.

Through our platform, we provide the tools, support and connections needed to shape a brighter, more inventive future, founded in Kenya and built for all of Africa.

What we stand for

  • Innovation first

    We put creativity at the centre of everything we build and every connection we make.

  • Trust & integrity

    Every interaction on our platform is grounded in transparency and mutual respect.

  • African-led growth

    We champion local talent and celebrate the diversity of African ideas on the world stage.

  • Impact at scale

    Small ideas can change the world. We give them the platform and partners to do just that.
