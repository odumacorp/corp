How it works

Both sides use the same app. Founders list projects. Investors find them and get in touch.

If you're raising money

  1. Create a free accountChoose "Innovator" when you sign up.
  2. Publish your projectSay what it does, what stage it's at and how much you're raising.
  3. Talk to interested investorsReply to proposals and book meetings in the app.

If you're investing

  1. Create a free accountChoose "Investor" when you sign up.
  2. Browse projectsFilter by industry and stage, and see how well each one matches you.
  3. Contact the founderSend a proposal or book a meeting.

What you can do here

Show investors what you're building, in one place they can come back to.

  • Publish your project with images, documents and your funding ask.
  • Apply for a verified badge so investors know who you are.
  • Receive proposals and reply to them.
  • See how many people have viewed your project.
  • Take courses, ask for a mentor or request consulting.
